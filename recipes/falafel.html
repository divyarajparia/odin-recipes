Falafel

Description

Flavourful Mediterranean dish made from chickpeas and spices. These crispy golden fritters are delicious on their own or served in pita bread with tahini sauce and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp asafetida (hing)
  • 2-3 tbsp flour
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Combine chickpeas, parsley, and cilantro in a food processor.
  2. Pulse until the mixture is coarsely combined but not pureed.
  3. Transfer to a bowl and add cumin, coriander, salt, black pepper, and asafetida.
  4. Mix well and add flour until the mixture holds together.
  5. Refrigerate mixture for at least 1 hour.
  6. Heat oil in a deep skillet to 350°F.
  7. Form mixture into walnut-sized balls and gently flatten.
  8. Carefully place falafel in hot oil and fry until golden brown (2-3 minutes per side).
  9. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
  10. Serve hot with tahini sauce and fresh vegetables.
