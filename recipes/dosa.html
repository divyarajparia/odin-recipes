Dosa

Description

Classic crispy South Indian dish made from fermented rice and lentil batter. Served with sambar and chutney, it's a beloved breakfast staple that's both savory and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal
  • 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Soak rice and urad dal separately for 4 hours.
  2. Grind soaked rice to a smooth batter.
  3. Grind soaked urad dal with fenugreek seeds to a fluffy batter.
  4. Mix both batters and add salt.
  5. Let the batter ferment for 8-12 hours.
  6. Heat a dosa tawa or non-stick pan over medium-high heat.
  7. Pour batter and spread into a thin circle.
  8. Drizzle oil around the edges and cook until golden.
  9. Flip and cook the other side until crispy.
  10. Serve hot with sambar and chutney.
