Lasagna

Description

Classic italian dish with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and creamy béchamel. A hearty and satisfying meal that's perfect for family dinners.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups spinach, chopped
  • 2 cups zucchini, diced
  • 1 tsp asafetida (hing)
  • 24 oz marinara sauce
  • 1 box lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Sauté spinach and zucchini in oil with asafetida until softened.
  3. Stir in marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions, then drain.
  5. Mix ricotta, 1 cup mozzarella, and parmesan in a bowl.
  6. Spread thin layer of meat sauce on bottom of 9x13 baking dish.
  7. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, and meat sauce. Repeat layers.
  8. Top with remaining mozzarella cheese.
  9. Bake for 25-30 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.
  10. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.
